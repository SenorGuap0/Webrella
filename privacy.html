Privacy Policy

Effective August 11, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Webrella Studio ("Webrella," "we," "us," or "our") handles information submitted through our website and information we receive while communicating with prospective and current clients.

2. Information we collect

When you use our project inquiry form, we may receive your name, business name, email address, and the project details you choose to provide. If you become a client, we may also receive business contact information, project content, website requirements, invoices and payment status, and other information reasonably needed to provide our services.

Please do not submit passwords, full payment-card numbers, bank credentials, Social Security numbers, or other highly sensitive information through our website contact form or ordinary email.

3. How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, prepare proposals, communicate about projects, provide website design and related services, administer Webrella Care, maintain business records, improve our services, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Service providers

We may use third-party providers to operate parts of our business. Our website inquiry form is processed through Formspree. Payments and recurring billing may be processed through Square. Hosting, domain, analytics, email, or other providers may also process information when those services are used. Those providers operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

5. Selling personal information

Webrella does not sell personal information submitted through our project inquiry form. We do not use inquiry information to sell lists of prospective or current clients.

6. Data retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed for inquiries, active projects, support, business records, dispute resolution, and legal or accounting obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website, email system, or online service can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have provided directly to Webrella, request a correction, or request deletion where appropriate. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate business, contractual, accounting, security, or legal reasons.

8. Children

Webrella's website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13 through our project inquiry form.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website, services, or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will show when the policy was last updated.

10. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to webrellacontact@gmail.com.

This page is intended to describe Webrella's current website practices in plain language. It is not a substitute for individualized legal advice.
