our story

Born from loud kitchens, long breakfasts, and the belief that coffee should never be boring.

Marea is a fictional café concept inspired by the colors, rhythms, flavors, and hospitality found across Latin America. The idea is simple: make everyday coffee feel like somewhere you actually want to stay.

That means bold drinks, playful food, a room full of personality, and absolutely no beige minimalist sadness. Come for ten minutes or stay for two hours. We are good either way.
