Our Story

Two friends. Two skill sets. One vision.

We have been friends for more than a decade and for years we have talked about eventually starting a technology business of our own. Webrella is our first real step toward turning that vision into something we can build together. We each bring different strengths to the business. Emerson focuses on design and connecting with clients to understand what they want from their website. Kidus focuses on development and the technical side of bringing those ideas to life. Together we want to create websites that look professional, work well, and genuinely represent the businesses behind them.

We are both students and Webrella is still at the beginning of its story. We see that as part of what makes this exciting. We are taking what we have learned, combining it with more than a decade of friendship, and building something of our own from the ground up.
